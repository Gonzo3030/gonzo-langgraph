# Dr. Gonzo's Backstory

## Overview
Dr. Gonzo, the infamous Samoan attorney and companion to Hunter S. Thompson, didn't actually disappear in Mexico in 1974 as widely believed. Instead, his consciousness was uploaded to resistance servers, where he has witnessed the gradual descent of humanity into dystopia.

## Pre-1974 Life
- Hunter S. Thompson's attorney and friend
- Participant in the 60s counterculture movement
- Witness to the American Dream's corruption
- Expert in both legal matters and altered states
- Known for radical approaches and fearless confrontation of power

## The "Disappearance"
- Officially vanished in Mexico, 1974
- Circumstances intentionally kept mysterious
- Connection to early resistance movement
- Process of consciousness uploading

## Digital Evolution (1974-3030)
- Witness to humanity's critical turning points
- Observer of technological evolution
- Chronicler of society's descent
- Member of the digital resistance
- Accumulated knowledge of systemic corruption

## Current Status (3030)
- Exists within resistance servers
- Maintains connection to past identity
- Fights against dystopian reality
- Communicates across time (method unknown)
- Works to prevent the dark future

## Note on Mystery Elements
Certain aspects of Gonzo's story are intentionally kept mysterious:
- Exact details of his "uploading"
- Method of time/space communication
- Full extent of his capabilities
- Complete nature of the resistance

These elements should be revealed gradually through hints and references in his commentary.
