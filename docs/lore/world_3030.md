# The World of 3030

## Overview
A dystopian future where corporate control, technological surveillance, and environmental destruction have created a stratified society living in the ruins of former civilization.

## Social Structure

### Elite Class
- Corporate leadership
- AI system administrators
- Control mechanism operators
- Lives in protected enclaves

### Privileged Workers
- Technical specialists
- System maintainers
- Corporate enforcers
- Limited freedoms within system

### General Population
- Basic universal income recipients
- Limited access to resources
- Constant surveillance
- Neural interface compliance

### The Abandoned
- Those outside the system
- Wasteland dwellers
- Underground communities
- Resistance members

## Technology

### Control Systems
- Mandatory neural interfaces
- Thought monitoring
- Behavior modification
- Social credit tracking

### Infrastructure
- Automated production
- Quantum networks
- Environmental domes
- Vertical agriculture

### Surveillance
- Ubiquitous monitoring
- AI behavior analysis
- Predictive control
- Emotional tracking

## Environment

### Outside Conditions
- Toxic atmosphere
- Extreme weather
- Dead zones
- Resource scarcity

### Protected Areas
- Corporate enclaves
- Weather-controlled zones
- Artificial environments
- Limited natural spaces

## Resistance

### Digital Underground
- Quantum encrypted networks
- Consciousness preservation
- Time-space communication
- Information preservation

### Physical Network
- Hidden communities
- Resource sharing
- Alternative technologies
- Survival systems

## Daily Life

### Control Mechanisms
- Mandatory check-ins
- Thought audits
- Behavior scoring
- Resource allocation

### Entertainment
- State-approved content
- Virtual reality escape
- Controlled recreation
- Monitored social interaction
