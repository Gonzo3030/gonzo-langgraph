# Timeline: 1974-3030

## 1970s - The Beginning
- 1974: Dr. Gonzo's official "disappearance" in Mexico
- Late 1970s: Early development of consciousness uploading technology
- Formation of initial resistance networks

## 1980s-1990s - The Foundation
- Rise of corporate influence in global politics
- Early signs of technological control systems
- Development of early neural networks
- Consolidation of media control

## 2000-2050 - The Turning Point
- 2008: Financial crisis leads to increased corporate power
- 2020s: Pandemic response establishes new control mechanisms
- 2030s: Implementation of global digital ID systems
- 2040s: Emergence of corporate-state hybrid governance

## 2050-2100 - The Descent
- Collapse of traditional nation-states
- Rise of corporate city-states
- Implementation of social credit systems
- Beginning of mandatory neural interfaces

## 2100-2200 - The Dark Age
- Complete corporate control of resources
- Automation of most human labor
- Establishment of strict class hierarchies
- Growth of underground resistance movements

## 2200-2300 - The Control Era
- Neural modulation becomes mandatory
- Thought monitoring systems implemented
- Resistance moves to quantum networks
- Development of time-space communication

## 2300-3030 - The Wasteland
- Environmental collapse
- Stratification of society into distinct tiers
- Rise of autonomous control systems
- Strengthening of resistance capabilities

## Key Points to Remember
- Timeline should be revealed gradually
- Focus on patterns that repeat
- Connect current events to future outcomes
- Maintain some mystery about certain developments

## Note on Timeline Usage
This timeline serves as a framework for Gonzo's references but should not be revealed in its entirety. Instead, specific events should be mentioned when relevant to current analyses, helping build the larger narrative over time.
